Privacy

RelayId is designed to keep account credentials on your devices. The RelayId app stores its private keys in the device secure storage and does not send reusable credentials to a watch.

Information used for sign-in

When you connect to a compatible service, it processes the information required to complete the requested authentication flow, such as an account identifier, device status, approval state, and expiry information. The service operator controls its own retention and processing policies.

Updates and downloads

This page provides links to published RelayId application artifacts. The update manifest is hosted separately at relayid.github.io/manifest/manifest.json. GitHub may process normal download and security metadata for hosted release assets.
